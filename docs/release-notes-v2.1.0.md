# Business Expense & Sales Tracker v2.1.0

This update makes the app easier to set up and use for business owners across different languages, while keeping financial data local to the Android device.

## What is new

- Choose from 10 app languages: English, Simplified Chinese, Spanish, Arabic, Hindi, Brazilian Portuguese, French, German, Japanese, and Turkish.
- Arabic includes right-to-left layout support.
- Complete a guided first-run setup for language, privacy, optional business name, and home currency.
- Change business name, language, currency, tour, privacy, and backup preferences from Settings.
- Follow a quick-start checklist for the first expense, sale, backup, and report.
- Use clearer transaction forms with required/optional labels and a persistent Save action.
- See a more responsive dashboard with clearer filters and an explicit operating-cashflow explanation.
- Use the new adaptive Business Expense Tracker app icon.
- Export expense and sales reports with the selected home-currency label.

## Privacy and compatibility

The app remains local-first, with no account, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or application backend. Existing expense and sales records remain compatible. INR stays the default for earlier installations, and upgraded users are asked to confirm their reporting currency.

## Safe update instructions

Install this APK directly over the existing app. Do not uninstall the old version first, because uninstalling removes app-private data. For additional protection, create a `.betbackup.zip` backup from the app before updating or moving to another device.

Requires Android 10 or newer.
